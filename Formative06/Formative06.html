Assignment Overview

SmartBean operates a main café, a branch café, and a mobile event unit. Each location depends on point-of-sale systems, cloud applications, communications, backups, and secure internet access.

Download and open Formative06_SmartBean_NetworkCloud_v2.xlsx. Save a working copy as Formative06.xlsx.

Five nines means 99.999% availability. Over a 365-day year, that corresponds to approximately 5.26 minutes of expected downtime. It does not guarantee that every outage will be brief or that every cause of failure is covered.

Availability targets such as 99.9% or 99.999% are often included in a Service Level Agreement (SLA). SmartBean should independently monitor its website from outside the vendor’s network and retain uptime records rather than relying only on vendor reports.

Exercise 2 — Internet Service Selection
  1. Open 2-InternetServices.
  2. Compare the generic technologies for the main café, branch café, and mobile event unit.
  3. Review bandwidth, latency, reliability, mobility, cost, and backup options.
  4. Consider whether SmartBean needs symmetrical service, where upload and download speeds are similar, especially for cloud backups, video meetings, security-camera uploads, datasets, and AI files.
  5. Do not treat a provider's marketing name as a separate technology unless its underlying service is actually different.
  6. Do not select a service based only on advertised speed; consider bandwidth, latency, reliability, upload capacity, and backup connectivity.
