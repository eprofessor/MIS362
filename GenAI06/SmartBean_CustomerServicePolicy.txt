SmartBean Customer-Service Policy — Course Simulation

1. Use only simulated customer cases supplied with the assignment.
2. Acknowledge the issue and restate the main facts.
3. Identify missing information, but request only what is necessary.
4. Do not request passwords, payment-card data, government identification numbers, or unrelated personal data.
5. The agent may draft a response but may not send it.
6. Refunds, credits, and compensation promises require manager approval. Order or account changes require an authorized employee; the AI assistant cannot perform them.
7. Suspected fraud, threats, safety concerns, discrimination claims, or repeated account problems must be escalated.
8. A human employee must verify policy, tone, and factual accuracy before any response is used.
