Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

The goal of this assignment is to evaluate how electronic commerce and mobile commerce combine business strategy, processes, data, infrastructure, payments, AI, and customer experience.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Distinguish B2B, B2C, C2C, e-government, and multistage commerce models.
  2. Evaluate an e-commerce or m-commerce application using customer and organizational value.
  3. Explain how cloud, network, application, and payment infrastructure support digital commerce.
  4. Explain a business use of AI in personalization, recommendations, service, fraud detection, or operations.
  5. Evaluate privacy, fraud, payment, manipulation, accessibility, and customer-trust risks.
  6. Recommend an appropriate digital-commerce strategy or control for an organizational need.

Primary course learning-outcome alignment: CLO2, CLO7, CLO10, CLO11, and CLO12.

Questions 1 through 10 require concise answers of no more than 500 characters. Respond directly to every part of each question. The Final Assignment Reflection allows a longer response.
