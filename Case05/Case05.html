Case Overview

SmartBean Coffee Customer Database

As part of its loyalty program expansion, SmartBean Coffee wants to centralize its customer, purchase, and rewards data.
Currently, store managers track purchases in spreadsheets and email updates to corporate weekly. This is inefficient, error-prone, and makes it impossible to gain real-time business insights.
You have been asked to explain the benefits of using a relational database system to manage customer data, and to help leadership understand how data can be structured, accessed, and kept consistent across locations using relational modeling concepts.
