Learning Objectives

In this assignment, you will use Excel to examine transaction processing, integrated enterprise updates, the procure-to-pay process, and ERP implementation readiness.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Explain how a TPS captures, validates, processes, stores, and reports business transactions.
  2. Use simple and conditional formulas to identify transaction errors and exceptions.
  3. Explain how an integrated enterprise system updates several business functions from one transaction.
  4. Identify inputs, processing, stored data, decisions, outputs, responsible people, and modules in procure-to-pay.
  5. Evaluate whether an organization is ready for a controlled ERP pilot.

Assignment Overview

SmartBean purchases ingredients through a process that begins with an ingredient request and ends with supplier payment. Each request, approval, purchase order, receipt, invoice, and payment is a transaction that must be captured accurately and coordinated across business functions.

Download and open Formative08_SmartBean_TPS_ERP.xlsx. Save a working copy as Formative08.xlsx.

The workbook contains a 50-record transaction dataset with intentional exceptions. Two example formulas are provided: one simple calculation and one more complex validation formula. Use Fill Down, inspect the results, and verify important exceptions before answering.

Detailed work belongs in the workbook. Webpage answers should remain brief and communicate the conclusion that resulted from the analysis.
