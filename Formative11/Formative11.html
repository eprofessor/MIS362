Learning Objectives

This applied review follows Summative11 and reinforces how a business problem is converted into a controlled information-systems project.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Review a business need, project scope, and human decision boundary.
  2. Identify accountable and responsible project roles.
  3. Evaluate whether requirements are measurable and testable.
  4. Review project work packages, constraints, dependencies, and checkpoints.
  5. Evaluate success measures and project risks before management acts.

Assignment Overview

SmartBean will review a limited anonymous occupancy and queue-measurement pilot at its current location. The project collects evidence to support management decisions; it does not autonomously make staffing, pricing, purchasing, or lease decisions.

Download and open Formative11_SmartBean_ProjectPlanning.xlsx. Save a working copy as Formative11.xlsx.

The workbook uses transparent project-planning formulas and binary 0/1 work-package selections so the focus remains on scope, requirements, feasibility, dependencies, risks, and success criteria.

Detailed review belongs in the workbook. The three webpage answers should remain brief and communicate only the final conclusions and strongest reasons.
