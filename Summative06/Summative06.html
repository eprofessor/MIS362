Assignment Purpose and Learning Outcomes

The goal of this assignment is to connect network fundamentals with Internet services, cloud platforms, connected devices, and access to cloud or network-hosted AI models.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Explain why organizations use networks for communication, collaboration, resource sharing, and service delivery.
  2. Identify basic network addressing and describe client-to-service communication.
  3. Trace a Hermes request from a student computer to a cloud or network-hosted model and back.
  4. Distinguish Software as a Service, Platform as a Service, and Infrastructure as a Service.
  5. Explain how IoT systems and cloud services support business processes.
  6. Evaluate privacy and security concerns when transmitting information to public AI services.
  7. Recommend when public, private, or hybrid network services are appropriate.

Primary course learning-outcome alignment: CLO3, CLO7, CLO10, CLO11, and CLO12.

Questions 1 through 12 require concise answers of no more than 500 characters. Respond directly to every part of each question. The Final Assignment Reflection allows a longer response.
