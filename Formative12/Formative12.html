Learning Objectives

This applied review follows Summative12 and reinforces how SmartBean should acquire, govern, and implement a limited AI-enabled information system.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Compare vendor-neutral acquisition and deployment packages.
  2. Identify the most important tradeoffs in a system-acquisition decision.
  3. Evaluate governance, security, accessibility, and human-approval controls.
  4. Apply critical readiness gates that override a favorable weighted score.
  5. Recommend whether SmartBean should begin, revise, or reject the proposed pilot.

Assignment Overview

SmartBean is evaluating how to acquire, deploy, govern, and implement an anonymous occupancy and queue-measurement platform that can prepare AI-assisted management summaries.

Download and open Formative12_SmartBean_AcquisitionGovernance.xlsx. Save a working copy as Formative12.xlsx.

The workbook uses one smaller vendor-neutral acquisition matrix, a governance-and-control review, critical readiness gates, and a final pilot decision. Critical privacy, security, accessibility, human-approval, logging, rollback, and portability conditions cannot be offset by a high weighted score.

Detailed review belongs in the workbook. The three webpage answers should remain brief and communicate only the final conclusions and strongest reasons.
