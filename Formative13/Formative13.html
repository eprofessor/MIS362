Learning Objectives

This final regular Formative assignment integrates MIS concepts by asking you to evaluate a future technology for SmartBean rather than merely predict which technology will become popular.

After completing this assignment, you will be able to:

  1. Evaluate a future technology against a defined SmartBean business need and the complete MIS.
  2. Compare conservative, plausible, and disruptive scenarios while identifying uncertainty.
  3. Create and evaluate a structured AI-agent prompt and response.
  4. Verify one important AI-generated claim using a reliable current source.
  5. Recommend whether SmartBean should monitor, prepare for, pilot, adopt, or defer a technology.

Assignment Overview

SmartBean wants to remain aware of technologies that may affect its customers, employees, operations, information systems, and strategy during the next three to five years.

Download and open Formative13_SmartBean_FutureTechnology.xlsx. Save a working copy as Formative13.xlsx.

You may select one of the six provided technology categories or choose another future technology.

A recommendation to monitor, prepare, or defer can be as strong as a recommendation to pilot or adopt. The quality of the evidence and reasoning matters more than enthusiasm for a new technology.

Detailed work belongs in the workbook. The three webpage answers should remain brief and communicate only the final conclusions and strongest reasons.
