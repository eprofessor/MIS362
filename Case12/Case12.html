Learning Objectives

This case supports the following course learning outcome:

  • Assess an information-system disruption and recommend incident-response actions, backup and recovery procedures, business-continuity measures, and resilience improvements.

After completing this case, you should be able to distinguish among:

  • Incident response: containing, investigating, communicating about, and managing an active incident.
  • Backup and recovery: restoring trustworthy data, applications, and services.
  • Business continuity: maintaining the most important operations while normal systems are unavailable.
  • Resilience: preparing the organization to withstand, recover from, and learn from disruption.

Case Overview

SmartBean Coffee Responds to a Major Systems Disruption

Early Monday morning, managers at several SmartBean Coffee locations report that point-of-sale terminals cannot connect to the cloud transaction database. The loyalty application is unavailable, and employees at the main office see a ransom message on a shared drive. An employee also reports approving a multifactor-authentication prompt that the employee did not initiate.

SmartBean's IT support provider disables the affected account and disconnects several devices, but leadership does not yet know whether customer, employee, or payment information was accessed. The most recent nightly backup appears to have completed successfully, although SmartBean has not performed a full restoration test in six months. Store managers need guidance on how to continue critical operations, what to tell employees and customers, and which systems should be restored first.

SmartBean wants a response that addresses the immediate incident and also improves backup, recovery, business continuity, and organizational resilience. Focus on management priorities and decision-making rather than detailed digital-forensics procedures.
