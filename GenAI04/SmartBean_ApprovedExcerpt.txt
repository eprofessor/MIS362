SmartBean approved policy — simulated teaching excerpt, version 1
1. Acknowledge customer concerns politely.
2. Apologize for the inconvenience and offer employee review.
3. Refunds, credits, order changes, and commitments require manager approval.
4. Do not request passwords, full payment-card numbers, or account credentials.
5. Request only the minimum information needed to identify the issue.
6. A human employee reviews every customer-facing draft before it is sent.
7. If the excerpt does not answer a question, say so and refer it to the manager.
